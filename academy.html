About BIOC
WHY WE EXIST

Cricket is not just a sport — it's a craft. And like every craft, it demands the right foundation. At BIOC, we started with one belief: quality cricket coaching should not be a privilege. It should be accessible, structured, and led by people who genuinely love the game.

We are not here to fill up a ground and call it training. We are here to build cricketers from the ground up. Batting, bowling, fielding, wicketkeeping — every facet, covered with intent. Because the foundation you build at 8 will define the cricketer you become at 18.

What sets us apart is not just coaching — it's discipline and ethics. Punctuality, respect, effort — non-negotiable at BIOC. We hold every player to a standard because we know they're capable of meeting it. Hard work beats talent. Always.

Academy Guidelines
BIOC GUIDELINES

These guidelines apply to every player and family enrolled at BIOC. They reflect what it means to train seriously and with respect for the sport, the coaches, and each other.

1
Attendance & Punctuality
Players are expected to arrive at least 5 minutes before their session begins. Warm-up is part of training — not optional.
If a player will be absent or late, the parent must notify the coach via WhatsApp before the session starts. Unannounced absences are noted.
Consistent absence or habitual lateness may result in a conversation with the parent and, if unresolved, removal from the batch to allow committed players to join.
Sessions are not held on declared public holidays. Schedule changes will always be communicated via the parent WhatsApp group in advance.
Fee PolicyMonthly fees are due by the 1st of each month. A 5-day grace period applies. Fees are non-refundable for absences — sessions are not deducted for missed days. Players joining mid-month will be charged on a pro-rated basis.
2
Conduct on the Ground
Respect for coaches, teammates, and the ground is non-negotiable. Disrespectful behaviour — verbal or physical — will not be tolerated under any circumstance.
Players must listen attentively when a coach is speaking. Interruptions during drills or instructions are disrespectful and disrupt the learning of others.
No foul language, arguing with coaches, or unsportsmanlike conduct. Cricket is a gentleman's game — we hold that standard regardless of age.
Mobile phones must be switched off or kept silent and away during sessions. Coaches will not stop a drill to remind a player to put their phone down.
Players who repeatedly disrupt sessions will be asked to sit out and, in serious cases, removed from the batch. Refunds will not be issued in such cases.
3
Dress & Kit
Players must wear proper cricket whites or sports attire — no jeans, school uniforms, or casual clothing on the ground.
Cricket shoes or sports shoes with good grip are mandatory. Slippers, sandals, or formal shoes are not permitted on the playing surface.
A water bottle is required at every session. Staying hydrated is a coaching responsibility — players who are dehydrated cannot train safely or effectively.
Players are encouraged to bring their own bat, gloves, and pads as they progress. Personal kit builds comfort and consistency.
⚠ Abdomen Guard & Supporter — Do Not Skip TheseAll male players — without exception — must wear an abdomen guard (box) and athletic supporter to every batting and fielding session. Players arriving without protective equipment will not be permitted to bat or stand in the field.
On Injury & Protective EquipmentOur coaches run sessions with safety as the first priority. However, cricket involves a hard ball and real athletic effort. Wearing the right protective equipment is your responsibility. BIOC and its coaching staff cannot be held liable for injuries arising from a player's failure to wear adequate protective gear.
4
For Parents & Guardians
We deeply value parent involvement — but the ground is the coach's space. During active sessions, coaches must be allowed to work without interference or commentary from the boundary.
Please do not coach your child from the sideline during a session. Conflicting instructions confuse the player and undermine the coaching process. Raise your points after — or in a Talk to Us slot.
If you have feedback or a concern about your child's progress — please reach out via WhatsApp or book a Talk to Us slot. That is exactly what those slots are for.
All communication from BIOC goes through the parent WhatsApp group. Please ensure notifications are on and messages are read promptly.
⚠ Parents Are Not Permitted Inside the School PremisesShantiniketan Educational Institute has its own access rules. Parents are not permitted to enter the school grounds during sessions. Drop-off and pick-up should happen at the gate. Formal trousers or pants are required at the gate — shorts are not permitted on or near school grounds.
5
Competitive Matches
BIOC conducts structured intra-academy matches every month across all age groups. These are planned, scored, and debriefed — not casual games.
Match schedules are shared in advance via the parent WhatsApp group. Players are expected to treat match days with the same commitment as regular training sessions.
Attendance at matches is strongly encouraged. Match experience is irreplaceable — it is where temperament is built and where a player discovers who they really are as a cricketer.
All match conduct rules apply — respect for opponents, umpires, and teammates at all times. How a player behaves in competition reflects BIOC as an academy.
Players who demonstrate consistent discipline, good conduct, and strong match temperament may be recommended for external tournaments and district-level opportunities as they arise.
